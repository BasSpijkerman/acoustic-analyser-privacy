Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the Resonote mobile application (the "App", formerly Acoustic Analyser) created by Bas Spijkerman. It explains what the App does with information on your device.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Resonote does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

The App asks for one permission:

  • Microphone Access: The App uses your device's microphone to analyse sound in real time. The audio is processed on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else.

3. Recordings

Only when you tap Record does the App save the microphone signal, as a WAV file. On iPhone it goes to the App's folder in your own iCloud Drive, or stays on the device when iCloud Drive is off. These files are in your iCloud account and on your device. We cannot access them. You can delete them in the App or in the Files app. On Android they stay in the App's own storage on your device, and are deleted in the App or when you uninstall it.

4. Local Storage

The App stores your settings, your microphone calibration, and a few counters (for example, when it last asked for a review) on your device. This data stays on your device and is not accessible to us. On Android, if you have turned on Android's own backup to your Google account, it is included there; recordings are not.

5. The App Store and Google Play

On iPhone, the App may ask the App Store, on your device, which version of the App you first downloaded and whether you have bought an in-app purchase. It may also show Apple's own prompt to rate the App. Apple handles any purchase and rating under its own privacy policy, and we receive no personal information from either.

On Android, the App may show Google Play's own prompt to rate the App. Google handles any rating under its own privacy policy, and we receive no personal information from it.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not use any third-party services that collect information about you.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page, with the date above.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at the email on the App Store or Google Play page.
